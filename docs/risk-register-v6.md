## How a row closes

1. The evidence listed is produced and linked in the row.
2. The owner and expiry are filled by whoever accepts the risk; an **accept-with-owner-and-expiry** row with a blank owner or expiry is not an acceptance.
3. Only then does the response change to closed, and the same facts are copied into `plans/PLAN_V6.md` §2 or the gate record. Gate V6-0G's "no critical risk left ownerless" and V6-5G's exception rule are checked against this register, not against prose.

## What this register does not do

- It does not decide any D-level question, approve an exception, or change a plan task.
- It does not add findings beyond §2; `docs/preflight-v6.md` holds the open decisions, and `docs/worker-delivery.md` holds the job-delivery evidence R5 leans on.
- Severity is the plan's priority, not a likelihood × impact score; no probability or cost figure is invented here.
